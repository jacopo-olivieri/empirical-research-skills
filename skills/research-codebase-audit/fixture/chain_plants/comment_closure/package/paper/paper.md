# Protein Intake Among Meal-Program Students in the Kestrel Valley School District

## Abstract

We measure daily protein intake among students eligible for the Kestrel
Valley school-meals nutrition program during the 2024–25 school year.
Program-eligible students consumed an average of 52.2 grams of protein per
day, above the district's 48-gram planning benchmark.

## 1. Program and sample

The Kestrel Valley school-meals nutrition program serves enrolled students
through two meal plans: the standard plan and the reduced-price plan.
Program eligibility covers full-year enrolled students, on both the standard
and the reduced-price meal plan; students who enrolled mid-year are not
eligible until the following school year. Our sample is the district's
student nutrition census, which records each student's meal plan, enrollment
status, and measured daily protein intake in grams.

## 2. Measurement

Daily protein intake is measured from the district's cafeteria intake
records, averaged over the school year for each student. The analysis sample
is built by `do/build_sample.do` from `data/students.csv`; Table 1 is
produced by `do/make_tab1.do`.
